About

Faith. Fellowship. Service.

Raider Catholic exists to help students encounter Christ, build authentic friendships, and live out the Catholic faith on campus and beyond.

Our Mission

We want every student to know they have a place here. Whether you have been Catholic your whole life, are returning to the faith, or are simply curious, Raider Catholic is a community where you are welcome.

What We Do

We gather for prayer, fellowship, service, conversation, and events that help students grow in holiness and friendship during their time at MSOE.

Who This Is For

Raider Catholic is for students seeking community, support, meaningful friendships, and a place to grow closer to God while navigating college life.
